ByteBite is an offline-first calorie and macro tracker for people who want a terminal-style food log. Search Open Food Facts or scan a barcode, commit portions, and earn XP while tracking daily targets.

• Swipeable card stack with today, log, plan, wish, and profile
• intake buffer shows daily kcal, protein, carbs, and fat against your targets
• catalog query searches Open Food Facts and merges results with a bundled local shelf
• scan frame reads barcodes with the camera or accepts manual entry
• commit card sets grams, slot (boot, runtime, shutdown, interrupt), and eaten today or a future cycle
• XP per eaten commit, combo multiplier when macros land within target band, levels, and streak with freeze tokens
• entry stack browses past days and lets you purge rows
• horizon shows planned packets across the next 14 cycles
• wish register stores barcode-unique items you can promote to commit
• score ledger shows level, badge grid, editable targets, and reset all data
• Onboarding sets daily kcal, protein, carbs, and fat targets

Nutrition data from Open Food Facts. Not medical advice. All data stays on your device.
