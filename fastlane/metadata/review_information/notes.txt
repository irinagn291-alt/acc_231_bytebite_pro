Camera permission appears when the user taps scan on the intake buffer card or opens the scan frame overlay. The system prompt uses the exact usage description from build settings: "ByteBite reads barcodes to score points for what you log." No other permission usage strings are declared. There is no login, no in-app purchase, no App Tracking Transparency prompt, no account system, and no account deletion flow. Users can wipe local data from score ledger via reset all data. Food entries are stored privately on device and are not shared or published.

ByteBite is a personal calorie and macro tracker with gamification. It is for people who log packaged food by name or barcode, track daily energy and macros against self-set targets, plan meals ahead, and earn XP, levels, streaks, and badges. No credentials or demo account are needed.

From a cold launch the app shows a brief loading state, then the boot sequence onboarding unless already completed. Swipe through four pages or tap skip to use factory targets. On page three, adjust kcal, protein, carbs, and fat, then tap boot. The today card (intake buffer) opens. Tap search for catalog query or scan for scan frame. Pick a product to open commit card, set grams and slot, choose eaten today or a future cycle hex day, then tap commit. Use the bottom row to jump between today, log, plan, wish, and profile. Swipe the top card away to reveal the next. On profile (score ledger), edit targets with write targets, view the badge grid, or tap contact bytebite.pro.

External contacts: world.openfoodfacts.org for product search and barcode lookup; bytebite.pro for a launch registration request and the contact-us page opened from profile. Product front images may load from URLs returned by Open Food Facts. On device: food log, targets, score, wishes, and cached catalog records persist in Application Support as an append-only event tape with snapshots. Bundled local shelf samples work offline. Behaviour does not differ by region; day boundaries follow the device calendar.
